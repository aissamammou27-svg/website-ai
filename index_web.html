Behind the Lens

Eléna Vance

"Photography is never merely about recording reality; it is the deliberate practice of extracting meaning from the quietest corners of existence."

Born in Lyon and based between Paris and Tokyo, Eléna Vance approaches photography with an austere editorial sensibility and an painterly reverence for natural illumination. Having studied under classical printmakers, she crafts images that balance rigorous geometric composition with profound emotional resonance.

Her commissions have appeared in Architectural Digest, Vogue, Monocle, and Hasselblad Masters publications, while her fine art prints are held in private collections across four continents.
